## The people before the explanation

Lorn Bay is a fictional community on Okanagan Lake. November 6–28 is the fixed story calendar, independent of the computer’s year. November 28 is the story’s Friday. Ordinary work, jokes, meals and obligations must remain worth playing after the supernatural explanation is suspected.

Seven years ago Wren left a town that had made her its daughter and its musician. She loved Sam and still left him, aged eleven, with a secret he could not safely carry. Dianne burned Wren’s goodbye note and allowed a presumed drowning to become the public account. Most residents accepted that account; they were not an organized conspiracy.

Wren now rents a flat elsewhere, repairs instruments for a living and has friends who do not demand a performance whenever she sits down. She finished the song. She returns to meet eighteen-year-old Sam on adult terms. She is not moving back, playing a homecoming concert, or undertaking to forgive everyone. Leaving was justified; giving a child sole responsibility for her secret was not fair to him. Her arrival includes delight, awkwardness, anger and uncertainty.

**October 16** is the receipt date of Wren’s return letter: twenty-one days before the protagonist’s November 6 arrival. It replaces the original November 1 stamp. Dianne’s reply is a different, **unsent page written after November 6**; its breakfast guest cannot predate the protagonist. Priya’s letter is a third document, from seven years earlier.

## Supernatural rules

1. The protagonist is a person made from the town’s remembering, shaped by fog and the repeated five-bar call. Their origin creates no debt to disappear.
2. ECHO theft transfers a **lived memory**. Its donor permanently loses access to that experience. A surviving document or explanation can teach facts again; it cannot restore remembering. Reconciliation never undoes the loss.
3. Listening, reading and accepting ordinary food do not inherently steal. The quilt choice explains its possible cost before commitment. Refusal leaves the memory with Dianne. Inspection spends no attendance slot.
4. Food and witness support FLESH and NAME. Offered work, shelter and companionship may sustain a life. These are not ending completion tokens.
5. Stopping the horn changes survival. Deterioration has narrated alternatives, an actionable warning, and a final offer of help. Collapse follows knowingly leaving company while deteriorating. A rewind restores an earlier warning state.
6. Photographs of the protagonist disagree by witness; voice recordings contain room tone. Other people’s writing can survive. Sam can type the chosen message and receive Wren’s reply, but cannot record the protagonist to prove their existence.
7. Nobody initiates touch before the separate-person ending. Wren offers her hand and asks; the protagonist accepts; Wren closes the space. Do not depict an earlier embrace in art.
8. The register supports local witness. Deliberately burning it severs local remembering of Wren; it does not annihilate her life elsewhere. The player understands the possible loss even to Sam before burning.
9. Confessions return the actual speaker’s fragment. All six are optional. Retellings of missed events award no fragments.
10. The sixth bar is not an identity password. Wren finished hers after leaving. The protagonist may make another answer, or live without playing one. The two-note interview fingerprint is not the sixth bar.

No ending is best, true, perfect or incomplete. Confession facts enrich music and details. Earlier theft facts remain even after reconciliation.

## Presentation and authorship

Painted places, ink keepsakes and readable recurring portraits. Nine typed locations support light, occupancy and objects. The creator approved the three opening paintings and acoustic direction on September 7. All nine places now have paintings, with separate quilt-loss, potluck, letter and bus-arrival compositions. A changed Kettle shows the protagonist’s work. Wren has a single definitive ink portrait introduced only at arrival. Further cast pose variants and sparse voice recordings remain production work.

Immediate prose is default. Optional pacing, text sizing, keyboard controls, artifact transcripts, recent dialogue and sound captions support different ways of reading. Reduced motion retains the paintings. All observations, consequential inspections, names and phone messages use the deterministic shared engine. No runtime-generated canonical dialogue.
