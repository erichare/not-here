The notebook separates observations, documents and questions. No observation labels a suspicion as established truth. Confession flags belong to the speaker; retellings and elapsed-time passages do not write them. Arrival supplies the reveal to an unaware player and preserves honest choices.

Reachability tests follow actual choices from the beach. State injection may test an isolated condition or audio identity; it is not proof an ending is playable. Donor-loss facts must survive hopeful endings.
