Swatches

A · stage-forward · recommendedThe place is the page; the prose sits on cold glass in front of it.

The procedural stage runs at full strength — beam, water, fog, the one lit pane. The reading column is a translucent cold surface with dissolved edges (no backdrop blur), so the weather still crosses the words. A thin strip carries the day, the snowline, and NOV 28; Barb’s book keeps the frame’s only amber. The sketch lives in a right-hand rail, top-aligned with the first line.

Strongest sense of place; quietest chrome. The Kettle’s counter lamp is the warmest thing you will see in a day.

B · ledger-forward · InkleThe prose is a page of Barb’s book, laid on the stage like a thing on a table.

A bone-paper leaf with faint ruling, a double hairline margin, and the day strip as the page’s own header rule — ink on paper, the pen’s blue around NOV 28. Choices are set in the hand. Documents are slips pinned to the page. The stage dims behind it (fog .6, lamp pools .5). The sketch is drawn in the page’s margin, where the cards always said they lived.

Most tactile; the book is the frame. The stage becomes atmosphere behind an object rather than the room you are in.

C · theatre · Kentucky Route ZeroNo surface at all: the words stand in the stage, the silhouettes stand large.

Prose is set left of centre directly on the scene with a dark halo, the set is scaled 1.3× so the wharf, the counter, and the dog have presence, and the strip shrinks to DAY 7 ● + hill + NOV 28. The sketch is anchored in the stage — by the light, by the counter — not in a rail.

Most cinematic; least “document”. It asks the most of each stage, and the generic places will feel thinner than the two heroes.

shared · the 3:12 beatNight interstitial

The column fades, the unit’s window frame drops away, and the wharf is there in night-deep. “3:12.” at the column’s left margin. Five pulses at bar length — a ring from the horn bell, the water brightening 5%, the column rattling a pixel — then 1.8 s of nothing while the beam crosses the silence. The snowline drops; DAY N+1 — MORNING re-inks in the strip; the morning place fades in. ≤ 8 s, any key or tap skips; under motion-off, 1.2 s still + the caption. Shown mid-beat, after the third bar.
