## Reproducible findings

### 1. Sam's duffel arrives after his trust is explicitly closed

**Narrative continuity concern; no implementation diagnosis.** The bag reaches the player's door without an observed invitation or reconciliation. The ending does not explain it.

Reproduce with these choices in the route above:

1. Day 6: tell Sam his recording is doctored.
2. Day 13: tell Sam to sit down in Wren's cadence.
3. Day 16: visit Sam. He treats the visit as evidence and says to shut the door going out.
4. Day 20 night: the prose says the player is outside his lit shed; whatever is being said inside is for the fog, which never used his sister's voice on him.
5. Day 21: ride Tam's bus and visit the empty house. At evening, the prose says Sam sent his packed duffel to the player's door, specifically not to the coach stop.
6. Day 22: the boat shed option is disabled because the player is not yet carrying what that door requires.

**Effect:** Sam's distrust was clear; his sending the player his bag was not. A visible reason or different delivery would preserve the causal thread.

### 2. “The clinic hours you never kept” can misdescribe a later clinic visit

**Low-confidence wording issue, not a confirmed logic defect.** Skip the Day 3 clinic, meet Priya at the hall on Day 6, then attend Day 9 clinic hours and claim the green wall. At Day 14, Priya reads back “the clinic hours you never kept,” before also confronting the green-wall claim made during the Day 9 clinic visit.

This can mean the original missed appointment, but “never” briefly reads as a claim that the player never attended clinic hours at all. Naming the first missed appointment would remove the ambiguity.

## Narrative observations

**Strongest sequence:** quilt theft, its callback, the potluck's rejection of Sam, and Barb's accurate read-back. Consequences changed people's behavior, and the register made the route feel remembered. The potluck supplied enough context before committing and a specific social cost afterward.

Loss was clear without audio: the quilt, missing lullaby phrase, Priya's inaccessible letter memory, and Dianne's lightness after her confession. Wade's half-eaten meal and loose coat made the horn's cost concrete.

Wren and Sam's departure and the final register entry gave the ending an unsettling price. The second confirmation made the final act deliberate. Barb sitting in her own chair and Moose making room supplied welcome relief. The paintings, especially the quilt aftermath, supported the prose at phone size.

**Pacing:** by Days 8–11 the morning choice, missed-visit report, supper, and 3:12 cycle was conspicuous. Several nights restate that the player is fed and someone else pays. Day 18's largely one-button town walk repeats conclusions established by the letters and thefts. A human reader should assess these stretches.

**Choice clarity:** morning limits and theft costs were clear. The disabled boathouse option conveyed an emotional condition without naming the missing action; that may be intentional. The plainly phrased final choices worked well after so much indirect conversation.
