## Fixed finding

The November 23 transition displayed November 22 / Day 17 night. Its new scene had not set the morning date before rendering. Commit `685dd38` adds that time assignment without changing prose, choices, stats, or story flags. A regression test reproduced the failure before the correction. The tests cover both reading and leaving the letter, plus resuming the transition. A neighboring dated transition is also covered.

The phone tester subsequently reached this transition through ordinary play and verified that **November 23 / Day 18 morning** agrees with the prose in the actual browser. [Corrected header screenshot](2026-10-20-evidence/mobile-date-fixed.png).

### Source triage for the later visual assignments

A separate read-only source review confirmed that **Two Wrens stays at the highway pull-in**. Arrival and honesty explicitly retain the idling bus, and the ending immediately continues that dialogue. Its explicit location in `packages/story/src/presentation.ts` should be `shelter`; Wren remains the character.

**Day 17 night is a solo sleep passage.** The ordinary/defended route belongs at the motel. An exiled player has a cot at the wharf ticket office, established after the potluck and retained on Day 17. Correcting this requires a state-aware location and no character portrait, so the wharf's usual Wade portrait is not substituted for Dianne. The blocked-door paragraph also says “back up the hill” on the exile route; that prose concern should be reviewed separately from the scene metadata.

No art assignments or story prose were changed during this test cycle. The date correction is the only implementation follow-up.
